# Vocabulary ledger — AENĒIS track

NOT SHIPPED. The authoring instrument required by LATIN-STYLE §2 and
AUTHORING-BRIEF ("Working rules"): before introducing a "new" word, grep this
file. **If an earlier capitulum taught it, the word is FREE** — no `nova` gloss
needed and it does not count against the capitulum's cap.

## 0. THE CHARTER OF THIS TRACK (designer rulings, binding)

The Aenēis is **not a third beginner door**. Its reader is *prōvectus*: someone
who has walked one of the two beginner tracks, or arrives already reading Latin.
Four rulings follow, and everything else in this file is bookkeeping for them.

### 0.1 The whole grammar ladder is open from page one

CURRICULUM §0 stages S1–S12 are all available in **a1**: subjunctives (purpose,
result, indirect question), *accūsātīvus cum īnfīnītīvō*, *ablātīvus absolūtus*,
passive, deponents, relatives, every tense. Nothing is gated. Sentence length is
free but must stay clear (LATIN-STYLE §2 caps stop at S9+).

### 0.2 What IS graded is the vocabulary

**≤ 10 new content CARDS per capitulum**, each one pictured by a scene spec and
recycled **≥ 3×** inside its own capitulum. The shipped counts:

| capitulum | cards | capitulum | cards |
|---|---|---|---|
| a1 Tempestās | 10 | a5 Equus ligneus | 9 |
| a2 Ad Āfricae ōram | 10 | a6 Sinōn et Lāocoōn | 8 |
| a3 Venus māter | 10 | a7 Trōia ārdet | 8 |
| a4 Rēgīna Dīdō | 10 | a8 Fuga Aenēae | 8 |

### 0.3 CŌPIA COMMŪNIS — what this track assumes without teaching

Because the reader is *prōvectus*, this track assumes an **Ørberg *Familia
Rōmāna*-level core** (est/sunt, videt, dīcit, venit, capit, currit, magnus,
parvus, urbs, terra, silva, arbor, mūrus, porta, domus, mēnsa, plēnus, vestis,
brevis, nox/diēs, rēx, deus, dea, vir, fēmina, puer, puella, annus, locus,
ambulat, portat, stat, sedet, vocat, quaerit, putat, nārrat, incipit …). Such
words are used unglossed and are **not** counted against the cap. Words that are
NOT assumed — poetic diction, epic furniture, proper names, anything an Ørberg
reader would still meet as new — are carded or glossed and listed below.

Where a word of the *cōpia commūnis* nevertheless carries the weight of a page,
it is **carded anyway** (silva a3, equus a5, nox a7): repetition with a picture
is never a defect. Such cards are marked *(cōpia commūnis, carded)* below.

### 0.4 Poetic diction is PRE-TAUGHT, never met cold

Every word of an authentic line is taught **before** the line is quoted, by the
`X quoque dīcitur: X = Y` device:

| poetic word | taught as | where | paid back at |
|---|---|---|---|
| ratis | `ratis = nāvis` | a1 p2 | Liber III |
| aequor | `aequor = mare` | a1 p2 | **a8, in `aequīs`** |
| fātum | `id quod deī fierī iubent` | a1 p2 | **a4, in `fātō`** |
| lītus | `lītus quoque dīcitur` (s.v. ōra) | a2 p1 | **a4, in `lītora`** |
| ōra | card | a2 | **a4, in `ab ōrīs`** |
| moenia | `mūrī quī urbem cingunt` | a4 p1 | Liber VII |
| arma / ēnsis | `scūtum et gladius`; `gladius ēnsis quoque dīcitur` | a4 p-proem, a7 p10 | **a4, in `arma virumque`** |
| dōnum | card | a5 | **a6, in `dōna ferentīs`** |
| pietās | card | a8 | the whole track |

### 0.5 The payoff: VERBA VERGILIĪ IPSĪUS

Each liber's **last** capitulum closes with authentic hexameters — one line per
page, each with its own scene and its own Latin-only marginal glosses, then a
graded-prose paraphrase, then a `versūs memorābilēs` page. A liber may also
quote **inline**, at the moment the line is spoken, when the line *is* the scene.
Text is OCT/Hirtzel–Mynors with quantity marks added and **not one letter
changed**.

| liber | inline | closing | file |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | Aen. 1,203 (a2) · the aposiopesis 1,135 "Quōs ego—" (a1) | **Aen. 1,1–3** (a4) | aeneis-al1.js |
| II | Aen. 2,49 (a6) | **Aen. 2,723–724** (a8) | aeneis-al2.js |

### 0.6 Categories used in the tables below

* **CARD** — a vocabulary card with a scene picture. Counts against the ≤10.
* **TAUGHT** — in story text with a `nova` gloss but no card. Counts against the
  ≤10 when it is a content noun/verb/adjective; the totals are kept small on
  purpose and are listed per capitulum.
* **CONTEXT** — used with no gloss because *cōpia commūnis* (§0.3) or because
  sentence + scene fully determine it. Free.
* **NŌMEN PROPRIUM** — a name. Glossed at first use, free against the cap (a name
  is not a lexeme to learn, it is a person to meet).
* **FORMULA** — a fixed phrase taught whole (`Memoriā tenē`, `Quōs ego—`).
* **VERBA VERGILIĪ** — a gloss that exists only to open an authentic line.
  Free against the cap by charter §0.5; listed so the audit can see them.

**CARDS (8):** sacerdōs · āra · serpēns · captīvus · hasta · monet · timet ·

**THE INLINE QUOTATION.**

```

```

Quoted **whole**, at the exact moment Lāocoōn speaks it — the same treatment
Liber I gives 1,203. Its two hard words are pre-taught in the prose of the page

liber's **closing** quotation is 2,723–724 in a8 (charter §0.5: the closing lines
must land on the liber's own picture, and this liber's picture is the carry).

**B RATING — LĀOCOŌN.** One dignified sentence: *dum sacerdōs ad āram sacra
facit, duo serpentēs ingentēs ē marī veniunt, et Lāocoōn cum fīliīs perit.* The

the page** — and the page after it shows the altar alone with its smoke. Nothing
is depicted, and no later page returns to it.
**ADAPTED:** Sinōn's long false narrative (2,57–198) is compressed into two

(*multa et falsa nārrat*), so a learner is never left holding a lie as fact.
**OMITTED:** 2,57–59 (the binding, kept as one clause) · 2,145–151 (Priamus'
pity) · 2,246–247 (Cassandra).

## Open questions for the line-audit

1. **Aen. 1,203 punctuation.** The draft prints `mittite; forsan …`; Greenough
   and several school texts print `mittite: forsan …`. The words are identical in
   every edition; only the editorial stop differs. Left as the draft had it.
