# Vocabulary ledger — HISTORIA SACRA track

NOT SHIPPED. The authoring instrument required by LATIN-STYLE §2 and
AUTHORING-BRIEF ("Working rules"): before introducing a "new" word, grep this
file. **If an earlier capitulum taught it, the word is FREE** — no `nova` gloss
needed and it does not count against the capitulum's ≤8 new lexemes.

**THIS TRACK IS A SEPARATE DOOR.** It shares no ledger with FĀBULAE. A learner
may open Liber I with zero Latin, so this file starts EMPTY and `Deus` (h1, p1)
is the first Latin word the track ever shows. Nothing from
`content/_ledger-fabulae.md` may be treated as free here — where the two tracks
happen to teach the same word (e.g. `labōrat`, `ager`, `videt`), each teaches it
independently.

Columns: `lexeme` — citation form · `how` — the gloss device that made it
comprehensible (imāgō, `=` synōnymum, `↔` contrārium, morphology pattern, or
"context" when the sentence and scene alone do it).

Three states, as in the FĀBULAE ledger:

* **TAUGHT** — in story text with a picture or a `nova` gloss. Free.
* **CONTEXT** — in story text with no gloss because sentence + scene fully
  determine it. Free.
* **FUNCTION** — high-frequency function word or copula form. Free against the
  ≤8 content cap (AUTHORING-BRIEF "Lexeme cap, clarified") but still glossed at
  first appearance and listed here.

## Liber I · CREĀTIŌ (S1) — `content/historia-l1.js`

Stage ceiling: nom/acc **singular** only, present of *esse*, 3rd-person present
of any conjugation (sg **and** pl, BRIEF ruling 1), `nōn`. The only ablative is
the receptive locative `in` + abl (`in caelō`, `in terrā`, `in hortō`, `in agrō`,
`in marī`, `in arbore`); the only accusative preposition is `in` + acc in h5,
glossed with a direction arrow. **No plural nouns anywhere in Liber I** — the
plural is Liber II's target.

Regiō II: **pictured before it is asserted** (many men, many animals, many
tongues), then glossed with the singular/plural pair.

## Additions register (what is on the page but not in Gn)

| where | addition | justification |
|---|---|---|
| h5 (Gn 4,3–5) | **altars** under the two offerings, one alight | Gn 4 says only "munera" and "obtulit". The altars are the traditional iconography and the only way to picture an offering; the fire that rises on one and not the other renders "respexit Dominus ad Abel … ad Cain non respexit", which has no S1 verb. Nothing is asserted in the text beyond `dōnum ārdet` / `dōnum nōn ārdet` |
| h5 | `Abel` staged with a flock (`grex`) | Gn 4,2 "Abel pastor ovium" — pictured, not named (`ovis` is not a lexeme) |
| h2, h3, h4 | Adam and Eva **robed in every frame** | DESIGN §8. Pre-fall the Vulgate says they were naked (2,25); this file simply never shows or says it, which is omission, not contradiction |
