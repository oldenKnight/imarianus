## Engine notes taken in Liber VII (binding on later librī)

1. **`crowdGroup` ignores `pose` and `seed`.** `js/actors-person.js` sets each
   figure's pose from its index (`i % 3 === 1 ? 'point' : 'stand'`) and never
   reads `o.pose` or `o.seed`. Several scenes in this file pass them; they are
   harmless no-ops, and no page's MEANING rests on a crowd's posture — where a
   crowd has to be doing something, a bubble or a second actor says so. **Do
   not author a page whose sense depends on `crowdGroup {pose}`.** Reported.
2. **`columna {broken: true}`** draws a jagged shear across the top of the
   shaft but keeps the Ionic capital above it. It reads as damage at scene
   scale; if the art is ever revised, the capital should go with the break.
3. **`solus` is on `js/boss-phases.js`'s FUNCTIO stoplist** (Liber IV's
   finding, still true): an authored boss gap on that word is dropped
   outright. Liber VII gaps `tuba · castra · leō · columna · templum · oleum`
   and none of them is on the list.
