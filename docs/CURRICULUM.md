# IMARIANUS — CURRICULUM v2 (authoritative content map)

One shared grammar ladder; three tracks walk it at different depths. A capitulum =
one content unit (7 steps, DESIGN §4). Nothing is gated between tracks; ladder
positions below say what grammar a capitulum may USE, which is binding on authors.

## 0. Grammar ladder (cumulative; a stage may use everything before it)

  S1  nom/acc sg (1st–3rd decl), present of esse + 1st/2nd conj, est/sunt
  S2  nom/acc pl, 3rd conj present, -que, et…et
  S3  genitive; dative sg/pl (possession, giving)
  S4  ablative + prepositions (in/ex/ab/cum/dē/sub), instrumental abl
  S5  imperative, vocative, questions (-ne, quis, quid, cūr, ubi), negative commands
  S6  imperfect; personal + demonstrative pronouns (is, hic, ille)
  S7  perfect (deliberately introduced, contrasted with imperfect)
  S8  comparatives, superlatives, adverbs
  S9  relative clauses (quī quae quod); 4th–5th decl consolidated
  S10 passive voice; common deponents (loquitur, sequitur)
  S11 subjunctive: purpose (ut/nē), result, cum-clauses; indirect questions
  S12 accusative+infinitive (indirect statement); ablative absolute; gerundive (light)
